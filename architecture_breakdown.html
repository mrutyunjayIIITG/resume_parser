Under The Hood: The ATS Pipeline Explained

Here is a visual breakdown of exactly how your ATS processes a resume, comparing the AI Engine against the Free Deterministic Engine.

1. WITH Google Gemini AI (The "HR Data Scientist")

When the API Key is present, Gemini acts as the ultimate reader. It can handle chaotic layouts, weird phrasing, and messy sentences with near 100% contextual understanding.

graph TD A[Candidate Uploads PDF] --> B(PyMuPDF / Tesseract OCR) B --> C[Extracts Raw Messy Text] C --> D{Google Gemini 1.5 Flash} D -->|Contextual Understanding| E[Reads Text like a Human] E --> F[Infers exact Start/End Dates] E --> G[Calculates Years of Experience based on Timeline] E --> H[Fixes Typos & Identifies implicitly stated Skills] F --> I{Entity Normalizer} G --> I H --> I I -->|Validates against Master Dictionaries| J[Database IDs Created] J --> K[(Saved to Supabase)] style D fill:#4285F4,color:#fff,stroke:#333,stroke-width:2px style I fill:#34A853,color:#fff,stroke:#333,stroke-width:2px
Pros:
  • Incredible accuracy on dates and experience calculation.
  • Can figure out if a candidate meant "Software Engineer" even if they wrote something vague like "Code Builder".
Cons:
  • Costs API credits per upload.
  • Takes slightly longer (~5-10 seconds) because of the network request to Google.


2. WITHOUT Gemini AI (The Custom Heuristics Engine)

When you remove the API Key, the system skips Google and relies entirely on the powerful Custom Heuristics we just wrote (Regex + Spacy + Master Dictionaries).

graph TD A[Candidate Uploads PDF] --> B(PyMuPDF / Tesseract OCR) B --> C[Extracts Raw Messy Text] C --> D{Custom Heuristic Engine} D -->|Regex Pattern Matching| E[Scans specifically for 'Jan 2020 - Present'] D -->|Spacy NLP| F[Guesses Names & Degrees] D -->|Keyword Scanning| G[Looks for Exact Skill Matches] E --> H{Entity Normalizer} F --> H G --> H H -->|Strict Enforcement| I[Deletes Spacy's mistakes e.g. 'JavaScript' as Company] H -->|Maps Valid Entities| J[Database IDs Created] J --> K[(Saved to Supabase)] style D fill:#FBBC05,color:#fff,stroke:#333,stroke-width:2px style H fill:#34A853,color:#fff,stroke:#333,stroke-width:2px
Pros:
  • 100% Free and runs entirely locally.
  • Lightning fast (processing takes < 1 second).
  • Protected from garbage data by the strict EntityNormalizer dictionary enforcement.
Cons:
  • Rigid. If a candidate formats their dates weirdly (e.g., Start: 12-20-19 End: 01-05-23), the Regex might miss it.
  • Can only extract skills that are explicitly written in the resume. Cannot infer contextual experience easily.
